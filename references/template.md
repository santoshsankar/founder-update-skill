# Founder Monthly Update Template

A monthly update has one job: keep your investors oriented enough to help when you actually need them. Investors who read a short, honest update every month can act fast when something changes. Investors who hear from you twice a year can't, no matter how much they'd want to.

**Cadence:** Monthly through Series A. Quarterly after, once the business has more structure to lean on.

**Length:** Aim for something that reads in under two minutes. If it needs a table of contents, it's too long.

---

## What [Company] does

One or two sentences. Assume the reader is juggling a dozen other updates and might not remember your model off the top of their head.

## Asks

Lead with this. Specific requests, not "let us know if you can help." Name what you need and, where it makes sense, name who you're asking. Investors act on specific asks and scroll past general ones.

## KPIs

The one or two numbers that actually drive the business, with month-over-month change. Revenue or the equivalent core metric, plus growth rate.

## Customers

Named wins and named risks. What's landing, what's churning or wobbling, and what's moving through the pipeline.

## Product

What shipped, what's next, and anything technical that's a real problem right now, not a footnote.

## People

Hires, departures, and an honest read on morale, not just headcount.

## Capital

Cash in bank, burn, runway or cash-zero date, and where fundraising plans stand if that's live.

## Major Goals

Two or three priorities for the next period. This is the section that forces the reflection. It's usually the hardest one to write honestly and the most useful one to read.

## Shoutouts

Who helped since the last update, and how. Skip this and you're spending the relationship without ever reinvesting in it. Name people specifically; a vague thank-you doesn't reinforce anything.
